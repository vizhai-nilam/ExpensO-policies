ExpensO - The Expense Organiser

Legal documents for the ExpensO Android & iOS app. Published by the developer of ExpensO (an individual developer based in India).

ExpensO is an offline-first, end-to-end-encrypted personal finance app. Your financial data stays on your device. The documents below explain what data the app processes, on what terms you may use it, and how to delete your data completely.

  • Privacy Policy
    What data ExpensO handles, where it lives, advertising & consent, cloud sync, and your rights by region.
  • Terms & Conditions
    The licence to use the app, no-financial-advice disclaimer, backups, advertising, and the one-time "ExpensO Full" purchase.
  • Data Deletion
    Step-by-step instructions to erase your ExpensO data completely, including any cloud-synced copy.
  • First-run Disclosures
    The plain-language summary shown in the app before you start using it.

These pages are also linked from inside the app under Settings → Legal. For a specific vulnerability or data question, use the contact details listed in the app's Settings → Legal screen.
