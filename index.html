Why offline-first

Most finance apps run on someone else's server. That costs more than you're told.

Your transactions, balances and payees get uploaded, aggregated and often monetised. ExpensO removes the backend entirely.

Privacy

A queryable picture of your spending sits on someone else's infrastructure — exposed to breaches, subpoenas, policy changes and ad-targeting.

Availability

Lose signal, let a subscription lapse, or watch the service shut down, and you lose your own financial history.

Lock-in

Your data lives in their schema, exportable only on their terms — if at all.

ExpensO is the source of truth, not a cache. Your whole ledger lives encrypted on your own device. Using more than one device? Sync through an encrypted log in your own Google Drive, if you choose to. There is no ExpensO server to breach, subpoena or switch off.

Personal edition

Everything you need to know where your money goes — and where it's going.

Six tabs in the side menu: Dashboard, Ledger, Budgets, Reports, Savings and Settings. Simple enough for day one, deep enough for years of data.

The whole month in one glance

Income, spending and the year to date sit at the top. Open Where your money went for a donut of every category, and tap a slice to jump to its entries. Upcoming payments for the next 30 days are right below.

  • Budgeted, Savings and Unallocated as three honest, independent numbers.
  • A side menu that opens in two touches: an icon rail, then the full menu with Reports and Data management.
  • Bell for reminders, and a + button for quick entry on every main screen.

Budgets that pace themselves

Set a monthly limit and ExpensO tells you whether you're under, on track, near the limit or over — as text and colour, never colour alone.

  • Rollover carries unspent money into next month automatically.
  • Hard caps warn you before an entry would breach the limit.
  • Nested budgets — "Transport" as a whole, "Fuel" tracked beneath it.
  • Envelope budgets — two caps on one category (Bike petrol and Car petrol) that pace independently.
  • Per-month overrides that stick forward, plus Copy previous month.

A ledger with three views

Day for today, Month for the running list, Year for a month-by-month Income / Expense table. Edit or delete inline — no extra screen.

  • Every row shows category › sub-category, payee, account and amount.
  • Log payees in any script your keyboard offers — Hindi, Tamil, Arabic and more.
  • Closed periods stay locked as a clean historical record.

Log it before you forget it

Every amount field is a calculator — never the system keyboard, never a stray decimal.

  • Calculator with + − × ÷ right in the amount sheet.
  • A searchable two-level category tree — search reveals a match wherever it's nested.
  • Expense, Income or Transfer on one screen; tags, payee and note optional.
  • Card numbers and CVVs are refused on entry, by design.

Savings goals with a destination

Name a goal, a target and a date. ExpensO shows the total saved across goals, each goal's percentage and the days left, recalculated whenever you open the screen.

  • As many goals as you like: a home, a car, an emergency fund.
  • Add money to a goal with one tap; "Goal reached" when you get there.

Employees & payroll that adds up

Tap the days worked on a month calendar. ExpensO works out what each person has earned, what you've paid and what's left — then books the payment as a real expense.

  • Pay salary in parts or as an advance; each payment lands in Invoices (SAL-…), the ledger and reports.
  • Reverse a wrong payment — the document and ledger entry are voided together.

Security & privacy

Not a promise — a description of what the code does.

Every claim below is enforced by the app itself, not by a policy someone could quietly stop following.

Encrypted at rest, always

An AES-256 SQLCipher database from the moment of install. Receipt images sit in a separate encrypted vault. Keys stay in your device's secure hardware.

Biometric-bound lock

The app-lock key is released only after a fresh biometric check — not a password ExpensO stores and compares.

No first-party telemetry

No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no usage tracking. The developer sees nothing about how you use the app.

Card numbers refused

A built-in guard checks every free-text field before it reaches storage and blocks anything that looks like a card or CVV.

Screenshot-blocked

Financial screens use the system secure-window flag (Android) and a resign-blur (iOS) so they stay out of app switchers.

Free, no account

Install it and start. There is no sign-up, no subscription and no ExpensO server holding your data.

Step-by-step guide

The manual, not the pitch.

The same depth that's built into the app — practical, in order, written for the first time you sit down and set it up.

1Set up your accounts

Open Side menu → Accounts → Add. Add one account per real place your money sits.

What to fill in

  1. Type — Cash, Bank, Card, Wallet, Investment or Liability.
  2. Opening balance — what's in it now. For a credit card, enter the balance owed as a negative number.
  3. Monthly salary (optional, bank accounts) — feeds the Dashboard's Budgeted / Savings / Unallocated split.

Deleting an account keeps its history in the Ledger; it just stops being offered for new entries.

2Shape your category tree

ExpensO ships with a ready-made two-level tree. Go to Settings → Category Manager, tap the pencil to rename in place (history updates to match), and add your own categories and sub-categories.

Category ids are stable once created, so renaming is instant and safe.

3Log your first entries

Tap + from Dashboard or Ledger and choose Expense, Income or Transfer.

  1. Tap the amount — a calculator sheet opens; confirm with ✓.
  2. Pick a category (search jumps to any match) and the account.
  3. If the category has more than one budget, choose which one this entry counts against.
  4. Optionally add payee, note and #tags.
  5. Save — it appears in the Ledger and every report immediately.
4Read the Dashboard
  • Income / Spending / Net — tap any for its exact value.
  • Budgeted — share of income already covered by a budget cap.
  • Savings — share booked under a Savings category; zero until you log something.
  • Unallocated — income with no budget claim yet. Not bad; honest.
  • Where your money went — tap a donut slice for amount, share and a jump to its entries.
5Create and manage budgets

Open Budgets → + Budget: name (optional), Applies to (category, sub-category or Overall), monthly limit, Roll unspent money and Hard cap.

For envelope budgets, create two budgets on the same category; each new entry asks which one it belongs to and each paces independently. Use Copy previous month to carry limits forward.

6Make sense of Reports

Choose a period, then read down: Cash flow, treemap, category ranking, trend, merchants, tags, accounts, budget vs actual, heatmap, recurring vs one-off and net worth. Open Cash flow or the treemap fullscreen to pinch-zoom.

7Set a savings goal

Savings → Add a savings goal: name, target amount, target date. A goal doesn't move money by itself — pair it with a budget under a Savings category so what you log there counts.

8Import, back up and secure

Settings → Import data reads exports from other apps, with a preview. Export writes a full JSON + CSV archive. Under Security, turn on Biometric app lock; financial screens are screenshot-blocked by default.

Under Appearance pick Light, Dark or System and a display size.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no ExpensO account and no ExpensO server. You install, set up and use it.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Because you hold the keys, nobody — including the developer — can recover data from a lost device. Export a backup regularly, or turn on Google Drive sync to keep an encrypted copy of your own.

Can I use ExpensO for my business and personal money?

Pick Personal or Business at first run. Each edition has its own navigation, categories and dashboard, tuned to its job.

Does it work with multiple currencies?

Yes. Money is stored as whole minor units plus a currency code, and each foreign entry freezes its exchange rate. Online rate lookup is optional and sends no account data.

Which platforms are supported?

Any Android phone or tablet on Android 12 or newer, from Google Play. iOS is planned.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Erase everything removes your data and encryption keys. Full steps are on the Data Deletion page.

Is ExpensO financial or tax advice?

No. It's a record-keeping tool published by an individual developer in India. Check tax treatment with a qualified professional.
