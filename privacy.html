Privacy Policy

App: ExpensO - The Expense Organiser  ·  Publisher: the developer of ExpensO (an individual developer based in India)  ·  Policy version: 4  ·  Effective: 2026-11-18

This Privacy Policy explains what data ExpensO handles, where it lives, and the choices you have. ExpensO is an offline-first, end-to-end-encrypted personal finance app: your financial data stays on your device. The app is free. It does not show ads today, but it includes support for Google AdMob, so ads may appear in a future version. If that happens, ads will only run after you agree through the Google consent prompt.

1. Summary

  • Your financial data (transactions, balances, budgets, attachments, …) is stored only on your device, in a database encrypted at rest (SQLCipher, AES-256). There is no ExpensO server; the developer never receives it.
  • ExpensO contains no first-party analytics, no telemetry, and no crash-reporting SDK for the developer.
  • Ads: none are shown today, and the ad software is not started. If ads are switched on in a future version, they will be served by Google AdMob. Google (and its ad partners) may then process your advertising ID, IP address (from which a coarse, city-level location is inferred), device and app info, and ad-interaction events, only where the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow permits it. Your ledger is never sent to any ad system.
  • Other ways data can leave your device are features you turn on: encrypted sync and backup to your own Google Drive app folder (ciphertext only), and an optional exchange-rate lookup.
  • You can export all your data and erase everything (including keys) from inside the app.

2. Who is responsible for your data

For data that stays on your device, the individual developer identified above is the nominal data controller / data fiduciary, but because ExpensO has no backend, you are effectively in sole control and the developer cannot access, restore, or delete it.

If ads are enabled in a future version, then for advertising data processed through AdMob, Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC acts as an independent controller (and, in some regions, as the developer's processor) under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the AdMob/Google Ads data terms. A list of Google's advertising technology providers is at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903.

3. What data ExpensO processes, and where

3.1 Data you enter (stays on device)

Transactions, amounts, dates, payees, notes, categories, tags, accounts and the last four digits of an account label (full card/account numbers are rejected on entry), budgets, savings goals, recurring rules, bill reminders, attached receipt images, and app settings.

  • Storage: a local SQLCipher-encrypted database and an encrypted attachment vault in the app's private storage.
  • Retention: until you delete the entry or erase the app data. Uninstalling removes it.
  • The developer's access: none. Advertising's access: none — no entry, amount, balance, category or attachment is ever sent to any ad system.

3.2 Advertising (not active today — Google AdMob)

ExpensO includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK so that ads can be offered in future versions. While ads are switched off, the ad software is not initialised, no advertising ID is read, and no ad request is made. If ads are switched on in a future version, then, subject to your consent (section 3.3):

  • Advertising identifier — Android Advertising ID (GAID) / iOS IDFA (the latter only if you allow tracking). Used to select and cap ads and to measure ad performance. You can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • IP address — seen by Google's ad servers on every ad request; used for ad delivery, fraud/invalid-traffic prevention, and to infer an approximate (city-level) coarse location. ExpensO does not request GPS/precise location and holds no location permission.
  • Device & app information — device model, OS version, language, coarse screen metrics, the ExpensO app id and version, and general connection type.
  • Ad-interaction events — ad impressions, clicks, viewability and (for install-attribution) SKAdNetwork/limited signals on iOS.
  • No financial data. The ad SDK receives none of your ledger content.

Legal bases: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); ePrivacy) where the UMP form asks for it; a US-state "Do Not Sell or Share / opt out of targeted advertising" signal is offered where applicable; DPDP Act 2023 — consent for a lawful purpose. Ads may be personalised (if you consent) or non-personalised / contextual (if you decline where a decline option is offered).

3.3 Ad consent — Google UMP and Apple ATT (only if ads are enabled)

Before any ad loads, ExpensO runs the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow:

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a GDPR/ePrivacy consent message asks you to consent or manage options (purposes and vendors) before personalised — and, in most configurations, any — ads are requested.
  • Where applicable in the United States, a CCPA/CPRA message provides a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" choice.
  • Elsewhere, a message may still be shown depending on local rules.

Your choices are stored on your device by the UMP SDK. You will be able to reopen the form any time from Settings → "Ad privacy choices" (shown where UMP requires it). If ExpensO is offered on iOS 14.5+, after the UMP step it will also show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you deny it, the advertising identifier is not used and ads are non-personalised.

3.4 Google account data (Google Drive sync and backup — optional, off until you turn it on)

If — and only if — you sign in with Google and grant the in-app cloud-sync consent:

  • Email address: used solely to bind the encrypted sync files to your account and to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth scope: drive.appdata only — a private app-data folder in your Drive that other apps cannot see. ExpensO cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • What is uploaded: an append-only log of encrypted change records (XChaCha20-Poly1305), plus a rotating set of dated, full-state encrypted backups written up to twice a day (the last ~14 are kept, covering about a week) as a safety net independent of the live sync log. Google stores ciphertext only; neither Google (readably) nor the developer can decrypt either the change log or the backups.
  • Restoring from a backup: reinstalling the app and reconnecting the same Google account can bring your data back, either automatically from the live change log or on demand via Settings → Data management → Google Drive Backup → "Restore from cloud backup." This only ever merges data into your device (last-writer-wins — nothing newer on this device is overwritten) and never reads anything outside the app's private Drive folder.
  • "Erase everything" and backups: the in-app Erase action deletes this device's live change log but deliberately leaves the rotating backups in your Drive untouched, so you can still recover your history afterward if you change your mind. To remove the backups too, revoke ExpensO's Drive access entirely (see the Data Deletion page), which deletes the whole app-data folder.
  • Legal basis: your consent. Withdraw it in Settings; revoke Drive access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3.5 Exchange rates (optional, off by default)

If you enable online exchange-rate refresh and grant the fx_fetch consent, ExpensO requests public, non-personal currency rates over HTTPS. The request contains no account data and no identifiers beyond the IP address any HTTPS request exposes.

3.6 Local notifications

Bill and recurring-transaction reminders are scheduled on the device by the operating system. No notification content is sent anywhere.

4. What ExpensO does NOT do

  • No first-party analytics, usage tracking, or telemetry for the developer.
  • No third-party crash/performance reporting.
  • Your transactions, balances, categories, notes and attachments are never sent to any ad system.
  • No sale of personal data for money. If ads are enabled in future, where a US state defines "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, that would occur only with your consent and you could opt out via the UMP form.
  • No precise/GPS location; no contacts, SMS, call log or health data.
  • No profiling or automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Data sharing and international transfers

  • The developer does not sell or share your ledger data with anyone.
  • Advertising (only if enabled in future): ad data would be processed by Google and its ad-technology partners, on Google infrastructure that may be outside your country. Transfers rely on Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards. See Google's privacy policy and the AdMob partner list linked in section 2.
  • Google Drive sync and backup (optional): you transfer ciphertext to your Google account; the content is end-to-end encrypted, so no readable personal data is exposed to Google or the developer.

7. Children

ExpensO is a general-audience finance tool, not directed to children. If ads are enabled, AdMob is configured with tagForChildDirectedTreatment not set to child-directed and a maximum ad content rating of "G". The app does not knowingly process data of children under 13 (or the minimum digital-consent age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has used the app, erase the data from Settings and contact the developer.

8. Security

  • Database encrypted at rest with SQLCipher (AES-256); attachments in a separate encrypted vault. Keys held in the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
  • App content excluded from OS auto-backup and the app-switcher preview (FLAG_SECURE / resign blur).
  • Sync and backup payloads use authenticated encryption.
  • No transport is perfectly private: sync and ad requests reveal metadata (timing, sizes, IP).
  • Keeping backups is your responsibility; the developer cannot restore lost data or keys.

9. Your rights and choices

9.1 Everyone

  • Ads: none are shown today. If they are enabled in future, change consent at Settings → "Ad privacy choices" (where shown), and reset the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Access / correct any entry in the app; export everything (Settings → Export); erase everything and the keys (Settings → Erase everything — see the Data Deletion page); withdraw cloud-sync / FX consent via the Settings toggles.

9.2 EEA / UK / Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP)

Lawful bases: contract/legitimate interests for on-device app functionality; consent for cloud sync/FX and, if enabled in future, advertising. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (including objection to direct marketing / profiling), and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. As a sole developer established in India with EEA/UK users, the developer would rely on Google's advertising SCCs for transfers if ads are enabled; contact the developer for any Art. 27 representative query.

9.3 United States (CCPA/CPRA and other state laws — VA, CO, CT, UT, TX, OR, MT, and others)

The developer does not sell personal information for money. If behavioural advertising via AdMob is enabled in future, it may be treated as "sharing" / "targeted advertising"; you could opt out through the UMP "Do Not Sell or Share" message or the iOS ATT prompt. You may request to know, delete, and correct; you will not be discriminated against for exercising a right. Global Privacy Control signals, where the platform surfaces them, are honoured by the UMP framework.

9.4 India (DPDP Act 2023)

Processing is on the basis of your consent for a lawful purpose (cloud sync, FX and, if enabled in future, advertising) or as necessary to provide the app. You may withdraw consent, seek correction/erasure, and nominate a person to exercise rights on your behalf. Grievances: contact the developer (details in Settings → Legal); if unresolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9.5 Other regions

Brazil (LGPD): rights of confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation, portability, deletion, and information about sharing; legal basis is consent. Canada (PIPEDA / Québec Law 25): consent-based; access and correction rights; the developer is accountable for transfers. Australia (Privacy Act / APPs): access and correction. South Korea (PIPA): consent for any advertising data; withdrawal and access rights. Residents of any other jurisdiction retain whatever rights local law grants and may contact the developer to exercise them.

10. Retention

On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall. Ad-consent choices (if ads are ever enabled): until you change them or clear app data. Encrypted Google Drive files: until you revoke ExpensO's Drive access (see the Data Deletion page).

11. Contact

Developer contact details are in the app under Settings → Legal.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the in-app version number is incremented and the acceptance screen is shown again so you can review it before continuing. The current text is always available under Settings → Legal, and this page is updated to match.
