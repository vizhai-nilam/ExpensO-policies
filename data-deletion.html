Data deletion

App: ExpensO - The Expense Organiser  ·  Effective: 2026-11-18

ExpensO - The Expense Organiser stores your data on your device and has no server-side account. There is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf, because the developer holds none of your data. You delete everything yourself, in seconds.

1. Erase everything inside the app

  1. Open ExpensO - The Expense Organiser → Settings.
  2. Tap Erase everything.
  3. Confirm, and pass the biometric / device-credential check.

This wipes every transaction, budget, savings goal, recurring rule, attachment, and setting, deletes this device's local sync log, and destroys the encryption keys in the secure hardware. It cannot be undone.

If you use cloud sync, this step deliberately does not delete the rotating backup snapshots in your own Google Drive (see step 3) — they are your own protected data, kept so you can still recover your history afterward if you change your mind.

2. Remove the app

Uninstall ExpensO - The Expense Organiser from your device. On Android this also removes the app's private, encrypted storage.

3. If you used encrypted cloud sync

The encrypted sync files — the change log and the rotating backup snapshots — live in your own Google Drive, in a private application-data folder.

  1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Select ExpensO - The Expense Organiser and choose Remove access. This also deletes the entire app-data folder ExpensO - The Expense Organiser created — change log and every backup snapshot.
  3. Optionally, before revoking, use ExpensO - The Expense Organiser → Settings → Export all my data if you want a copy first, or Settings → Data management → Google Drive Backup → Backup now if you want one more up-to-date snapshot kept.

4. Nothing else exists

ExpensO - The Expense Organiser has no analytics store, no logs, and no third-party processors holding your data. The only copies anywhere are the ones you chose to create (cloud sync / backup to your own Google Drive, or an export file you saved). After steps 1–3 no copy of your ExpensO - The Expense Organiser data remains anywhere.
