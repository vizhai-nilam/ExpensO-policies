Before you start

The plain-language summary shown inside the app on first launch.

ExpensO - The Expense Organiser keeps your money data on your device, in an encrypted database. There is no ExpensO account and no ExpensO server — the developer never sees your entries.

What this means

  • Private by default. Nothing you enter leaves your device unless you turn on a sync feature yourself. Your transactions, balances and attachments are never sent to any advertising or analytics system.
  • The free version is ad-supported. ExpensO shows ads through Google AdMob, and only after you answer the Google consent prompt (and, on iOS, the Apple tracking prompt). For ads, Google may use an advertising ID, your IP address (for a rough city-level location) and ad-interaction data — never your financial data. You can change these choices any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • One-time "ExpensO Full" removes every ad. When you buy it, the ad software is never started — no ad requests, no advertising ID, nothing.
  • No first-party tracking. ExpensO itself has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash-reporting for the developer.
  • You hold the keys. The encryption keys live only in your device's secure hardware. If you erase the app or lose the device, your data cannot be recovered by anyone — keep your own backups (Export, or encrypted sync to your own Google Drive).
  • Not financial advice. ExpensO shows you what you enter. It does not advise you on money decisions.

Permissions ExpensO may ask for

Camera (scan pairing QR, capture receipts) · Local network (find your other devices) · Biometrics (unlock, confirm export/erase) · Notifications and alarms (bill reminders) · Advertising ID (serve ads on the free tier) · Internet.

Your choices

  • Buy ExpensO Full to remove all ads.
  • Change ad consent in Settings → "Ad privacy choices"; on iOS also in the system Tracking settings. Reset the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Turn sync and exchange rates on or off any time in Settings.
  • Export everything as JSON + CSV from Settings.
  • Erase everything (data + keys) from Settings.
  • Read the full Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy any time under Settings → Legal.
